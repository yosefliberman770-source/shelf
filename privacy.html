Shelfmark Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

Shelfmark does not collect, store or share any personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no server.

Your data

Everything you enter (items, folders, sessions, notes and settings) is stored only on your device. It is not sent to the developer or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes this data. Backups you export are files you control.

Book lookup and covers

If you tap Find when adding a book, the title, author or ISBN you typed is sent to Open Library (openlibrary.org, run by the Internet Archive) to look up details. Book covers are loaded from Open Library's cover service using the book's ISBN or cover number. Nothing else is sent. Open Library's own privacy policy applies to those requests.

Goodreads import

If you import a Goodreads export file, it is read on your device and never uploaded anywhere.

Notifications

Daily reminders are scheduled on your device. No notification data leaves your phone.

Children

Shelfmark collects no data from anyone, including children.
