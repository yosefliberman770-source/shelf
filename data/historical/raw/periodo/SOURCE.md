# PeriodO: a gazetteer of period definitions

- **Official source:** PeriodO project (Ryan Shaw, Adam Rabinowitz and others).
- **Dataset page:** https://perio.do/
- **Version / date:** Full dataset (d.json) as downloaded.
- **Licence:** Creative Commons CC0 1.0 — https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/ (see LICENSE.md)
- **Geographic coverage:** Worldwide.
- **Historical date range:** All periods.
- **Download date:** 2026-09-30
- **Total size:** 7.4 MB in 1 files

## What it contains

Scholarly definitions of named periods (e.g. "Late Antique", "High Middle Ages" for a region), each with its source, spatial coverage and start/end ranges.

## Known limitations

Reference only: kept to check what a period name means for a region. Not yet used by the app.
