# Repertoriul Arheologic Național (RAN) — national archaeological register of Romania

- **Official source:** Institutul Național al Patrimoniului / CIMEC. List pages from ran.cimec.ro; positions from the public ArcGIS service behind map.cimec.ro.
- **Dataset page:** https://ran.cimec.ro/
- **Version / date:** Snapshot 2026-09-30: 29,052 sites (59 list pages of 500, ordered by period) and their positions.
- **Licence:** Open Government Licence as listed on data.gov.ro (seen through a search result; data.gov.ro refuses connections from this environment). Not verified. — https://data.gov.ro/ (see LICENSE.md)
- **Geographic coverage:** Romania.
- **Historical date range:** All periods; Shelf keeps sites with a migration-period or medieval component (400–1600), dated by the stated century where given.
- **Download date:** 2026-09-30
- **Total size:** 101.6 MB in 89 files

## What it contains

Site code, name, category, type, county, locality, components and period; point positions.

## Processed into Shelf

2,865 dated sites → private data pack (settlements, churches, monasteries, fortifications, sites).
