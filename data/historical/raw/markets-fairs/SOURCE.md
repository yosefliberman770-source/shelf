# Gazetteer of Markets and Fairs in England and Wales to 1516 (database)

- **Official source:** Samantha Letters, Centre for Metropolitan History (IHR); SAS-Space repository.
- **Dataset page:** https://sas-space.sas.ac.uk/106/
- **Version / date:** SAS-Space deposit (files: MarketsFairs.mdb, MFEngland.txt, MFWales.txt, guide).
- **Licence:** Not stated — SAS-Space marks the files "UNSPECIFIED". Licence not verified. — https://sas-space.sas.ac.uk/106/ (see LICENSE.md)
- **Geographic coverage:** England and Wales.
- **Historical date range:** To 1516.
- **Download date:** 2026-09-30
- **Total size:** 1.3 MB in 3 files

## What it contains

2,395 places, 2,604 markets and 2,934 fairs with OS grid references, grant/charter year, first record, type of evidence, borough status, mint and 1334 valuation.

## Processed into Shelf

Local-only tiles (local-sites.pmtiles), layer "Market rights & fairs" in local builds.
