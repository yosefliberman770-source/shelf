- **Version / date:** API snapshot (5,522 places in 56 pages of 100).
- **Licence:** Not stated for the data (only photographs are marked CC BY 4.0). Licence not verified. — https://maps-of-power.oeaw.ac.at/ (see LICENSE.md)
- **Geographic coverage:** Byzantine Balkans and Greece (TIB volumes), with medieval church case studies elsewhere (e.g. Slovakia).

## What it contains

Places in Linked Places format: names, types (urban/rural settlement, fortification, church, monastery…), attestation timespans, geometry (points or areas).
