## What it contains

Navigable rivers and canals: direct evidence (documents, finds, canal building), indirect evidence (mainly place-names), heads of navigation, and place-names relating to river traffic — each a shapefile in a ZIP; plus the general guide (PDF) and an overview map (PNG).
