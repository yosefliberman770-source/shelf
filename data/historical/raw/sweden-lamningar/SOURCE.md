# Lämningar i Sverige — ancient remains of Sweden (Kulturmiljöregistret)

- **Official source:** Riksantikvarieämbetet (Swedish National Heritage Board), pub.raa.se.
- **Dataset page:** https://pub.raa.se/nedladdning/datauttag/lamningar_v1/
- **Version / date:** GeoPackage of 2026-09-30.
- **Licence:** Not stated on the download page; not verified. — https://www.raa.se/hitta-information/oppna-data/ (see LICENSE.md)
- **Geographic coverage:** Sweden.
- **Historical date range:** All periods; Shelf keeps remains dated to the Iron Age or Middle Ages.
- **Download date:** 2026-09-30
- **Total size:** 2.1 GB in 1 files

## What it contains

Remains with type, dating and geometry.

## Processed into Shelf

Iron Age / medieval remains → private data pack.
