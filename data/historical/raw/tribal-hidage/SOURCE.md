- **Historical date range:** c. AD 450–650.
- **Download date:** 2026-09-29
- **Total size:** 4.1 MB in 5 files

## What it contains

Early Anglo-Saxon burial census: 834 burial sites (Sites_data.csv, with coordinates), 12,379 buried individuals (Individuals_Data.csv) and 26,043 grave objects (Objects_Data.csv); a general guide (PDF) and a plot of burial sites (JPG). The collection has no kingdom boundary polygons — only these census tables.
