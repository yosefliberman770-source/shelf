# Key to English Place-Names (KEPN)

**EXTERNAL REFERENCE ONLY — no legitimate bulk download.**

- **Why:** The official site (https://kepn.nottingham.ac.uk/, Institute for Name-Studies, University of Nottingham) offers search and browse only. No download, export or API is offered; the site says "© 2026 University of Nottingham. All Rights Reserved"; and its robots.txt disallows automated access to everything except /about. Scraping it would break those terms.
- **What to do:** None. Use it as an online source (link to its search page for a place-name). If bulk use is ever needed, ask the Institute for Name-Studies for permission.
- **Licence:** All rights reserved.
- **Geographic coverage:** England (pre-1974 counties).
- **Historical date range:** Names of cities, towns and villages, most of them "well over a thousand years old" (KEPN about page).
- **Checked on:** 2026-09-29
