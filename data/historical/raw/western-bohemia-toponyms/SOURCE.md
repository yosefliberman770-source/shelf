- **Historical date range:** Attestations up to 1500.
- **Download date:** 2026-09-30
- **Total size:** 459.6 KB in 5 files

## What it contains

1,935 place names with Czech and German names, first and second attestation years and sources, original written form, etymology, coordinates.

## Processed into Shelf

Joined to Wikidata settlements with the same Czech name within 2 km (980: adds the attested form and, when earlier, the first-mention year) or added as gazetteer `wbohemia` (839).
